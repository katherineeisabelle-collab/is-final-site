Why I love it

Tennis is one of my favorite ways to move, reset mentally, and build discipline. It’s challenging in a fun way because every point is a new problem to solve.

My journey

I’ve learned that progress in tennis is about consistency: short practice sessions, solid fundamentals, and learning from mistakes point by point.
